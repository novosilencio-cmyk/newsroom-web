From line to relief

An eye holds your gaze. Around it, the lines keep finding new paths.

In Bjørn Moe Aldema’s drawing from 2005, lines wind around a large eye, gather into spirals and follow one another across the page. Here they gain a spatial dimension in a digital relief.
