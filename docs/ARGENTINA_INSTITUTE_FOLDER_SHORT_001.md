# Argentina — concise institute folder 001

Status: INTERNAL DRAFT / NOT PUBLISHED / NOT SENT

## One recommendation

Run one bounded Argentina capacity pilot that asks how fiscal stability can be protected while more of the country's existing human, educational, cultural, productive and natural capacity is activated to improve everyday life and future income.

## Why this is worth testing

Argentina already has valuable capabilities in education, research, knowledge services, regional production, food, culture, tourism, craft and natural diversity. The question is not only where expenditure can be reduced, but where short-term savings may remove future options — and where better connections between existing capacities can create value.

## What the pilot examines

- education, research and pathways into meaningful work;
- knowledge-economy and export capacity;
- domestic production that meets real Argentine needs;
- local refinement: food, furniture, craft, repair, hospitality and regional production;
- housing, mobility and life-course enabling infrastructure;
- welfare as safety net and possible capacity-restoring infrastructure;
- cleaner materials and production that preserve the good functions of modern life while reducing avoidable toxicity, waste and shifted future cost;
- accountable public responsibility: mandate, expertise, evidence, implementation and outcome.

## Concrete demonstration product

### Argentine Living Culture & Discovery Atlas

Pilot 5–10 different places and connect food, local production, arts, learning, history, nature, activities, accommodation, events and transport into living place portraits.

The test is simple: does this reveal useful local capacity and economic/cultural connections that existing platforms do not make sufficiently visible? If not, do not scale it.

## What we can contribute

Bounded research, capacity analysis, alternative-scenario design, pilot architecture, implementation support, agent-assisted knowledge organisation, training and outcome evaluation.

Journalism remains editorially independent from any paid follow-on work. Analysis does not inherit democratic mandate.

## Suggested next step

Agree one small pilot, define evidence and success criteria in advance, run it with Argentine public/local sources and participants, and decide from the result whether a larger programme is justified.
