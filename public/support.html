Support the whole

What support can build

From keeping the work running to building editorial capacity

The first task is to sustain the basic work: publishing, research, source work, digital tools and necessary operating costs. With stronger and more stable support, Experimental Newsroom aims to expand what it can do — including establishing a physical workspace, commissioning journalists and other contributors, and eventually employing journalists when the finances can responsibly carry those commitments.

The ambition is not simply to maintain a website. It is to build independent journalistic capacity for examining how society works, what produces results, what it costs, who is affected and what remains unresolved.

Editorial independence

Support does not buy coverage

Contributions do not purchase editorial influence, favourable reporting or inclusion in guides, recommendations or investigations. Donors cannot decide which stories we investigate, what conclusions we reach, whom we cover or what we publish.
