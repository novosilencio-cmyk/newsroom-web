# Reader support — 13 October 2026

Experimental Newsroom offers voluntary one-time support for its own journalism. The reader chooses an amount in NOK. Stripe hosts the payment, with the same-language contribution title, independence statement and confirmation. Contributions are marked JOURNALISM in checkout and payment records. The existing full-refund policy remains: request within 14 days of payment; normally processed within 14 days of receiving the request.

## Reader journey

The three front-page entrances link to the appropriate support page. Each article has a quiet link after its text. The support page explains purpose, then currency and the payment action, editorial independence, refunds, contact and privacy. Its language selector is explicit and works without JavaScript. Each language has its own canonical URL and reciprocal hreflang links. English is the international fallback. There is no country-based redirect, subscription, special access or promised editorial influence.

## Comparison

The previous support page mixed three possible purposes with incomplete payment status. The new page makes journalism the available payment purpose while preserving Atriet and the wider project as separate future options. Article-body text, claims, sources, attribution and existing article dates are preserved. The navigation and CSS additions are reviewed separately from those earlier article reviews, which remain in each updated receipt's previous_review field.

## Six stages

1. Whole impression: a clear choice to support freely readable journalism.
2. Strengths: voluntary amount, independent newsroom, named recipient and established refund promise.
3. Style: plain, calm service copy rather than repeated sales prompts; Argentine Spanish uses vos forms.
4. Weaknesses: the former page did not distinguish available journalism payment from future purposes and lacked language-specific entry points.
5. Refinement: eight explicit language URLs, same-language Stripe titles and confirmation, accessible focus states and one payment action per page.
6. Final version: the exact generated page hashes are recorded in language-reviews.json. Existing prose reviews are retained; the navigation-only changes are version-bound to the new output.

The loaded modules were Ariadne's standard repertoire, editorial transfer, language harvest and Norwegian–English semantics, read on 13 October 2026. This is same-assistant language and semantic review, with no claim of independent or native-speaker certification. The complete support terms and privacy notice remain in Norwegian, clearly labelled in every language. The translated support pages summarize the actual one-time flow without changing the terms.

## Semantic control

Each language preserves: the journalism purpose, voluntary contribution, no product or privileged access, editorial independence, single payment, NOK currency, possible conversion by the payer's provider, the 14-day refund request window, original-method refund and telephone contact. Optional future employment is conditional on finances. Traditional Chinese is included alongside simplified Chinese. Portuguese for Brazil is the next requested language and has no active page or payment link in this release.

## Validation

No real payment or bank settlement is represented as tested.
