Level 2 · news entry and public facts

Enter the news through public facts

Turn one bounded public source set into a clear opening—without letting urgency outrun verification.

Originator & idea-maker: Mr.BM.Benlolo · Human adaptation and editorial development: Experimental Newsroom human–AI collaboration · Published: 2 September 2026

You need

One dated public document and its official context.

You will make

A lead, nut paragraph, source-status line and negative control.

Time

About 45 minutes, plus optional reader feedback.

Before you register

What the course contains

  1. Freeze the source set: record issuer, version, date and document type.
  2. Choose a consequential fact: identify what changes for whom.
  3. Lead and nut paragraph: orient without borrowing the source’s preferred drama.
  4. Claim carriers: keep records, attributed claims and analysis distinct.
  5. Uncertainty line: name the next factual test.
  6. Negative control: expose the dramatic lead the evidence cannot carry.

TRY

Public sample exercise

What the source establishes—and what it cannot

Choose one public document. Record its title, issuer, publication date and version. Then write two sentences:

  1. “This source establishes…”
  2. “It does not establish…”

Boundary check

A proposal is not a decision. A decision is not implementation. An institution’s prediction is not an observed effect. Keep the document’s status in the grammar of both sentences.

Method and standard

Response, evidence, insight and limitation

The course asks what response is being tried, what evidence establishes its current state, what insight might travel, and which limitation must remain visible. Multiple perspectives are a reporting requirement when consequences or interpretations differ—not decorative balance.
