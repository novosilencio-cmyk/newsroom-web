# Trivselskretsløp — KFM/motlesning + internasjonalt sammenligningssøk 001

Status: INTERNAL CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL / NOT PUBLISHED / NOT SENT
Origin: Bjørn Moe Aldema
Purpose: challenge and refine the candidate field `nasjonale og lokale trivselskretsløp` before it materially shapes the Argentina booklet or cross-project societal-capacity services.

## Core candidate

A national/local wellbeing value loop asks how human, cultural, productive, material and natural capacities can be connected so that useful value, skills, belonging and wellbeing circulate and deepen locally/nationally without making autarky, protectionism, volume growth or consumption maximisation the objective.

## International comparison — what survives

### Preston / Community Wealth Building
Strong support for analysing procurement and anchor-institution spending as local-capacity levers. Evidence indicates local procurement can retain more spending locally and may support employment, wages, wellbeing and smaller suppliers without necessarily increasing procurement cost. Limit: local procurement is not sufficient by itself; civic participation and community ownership matter.

### Scotland
Community Wealth Building is explicitly connected to a wellbeing economy, local supply chains, jobs, ownership, finance and anchor institutions. Useful transfer: procurement, land/assets, workforce, ownership and finance should be separate levers rather than one undifferentiated 'buy local' rule.

### New Zealand
The Living Standards Framework is useful as a control against narrow economic optimisation: assess multiple wellbeing dimensions, distribution, long-term effects and cultural/children's wellbeing. Transfer: trivselskretsløp must remain multidimensional and evidence-led.

### Japan
Regional revitalisation around local speciality products supports the idea that local resources + story + community participation can create durable regional identity and economic activity. One Village One Product adds a useful contrast: focus and quality can matter more than indiscriminate product proliferation. Warning: naming something 'local' does not make it successful.

### South Korea
Local-currency research supports the hypothesis that reducing leakage and increasing local circulation can matter, but consumer attitudes, norms and actual substitution behaviour condition impact. Transfer: attention, pride and behaviour are legitimate variables to study, but not assume.

### France
Territorial food projects join producers, processors, distributors, public bodies and consumers around social, environmental, economic and health goals. Transfer: sector-specific territorial loops may be more implementable than one national master-loop.

### Italy
Village/cultural revitalisation programmes combine heritage, public space, cultural activity, small enterprise, tourism and local social fabric. Transfer: culture can be a cross-cutting development capacity, but tourism must preserve rather than consume the place.

### OECD culture/local development
Culture can support local economic development, inclusion, health and wellbeing and act as a catalyst for other activities. Transfer: culture should not be treated as decorative content or merely tourism demand.

## Motlesning — strongest objections

1. LOCAL != BETTER. Local production can be inefficient, low quality, unsafe or ecologically worse than imports.
2. NATIONAL PRIORITY != HOSTILITY. Prioritising national/local capacity is legitimate; the control is whether it creates unnecessary harm, rights violations, exclusion or destructive externalisation.
3. CIRCULATION != VALUE CREATION. Money moving locally is not enough; useful output, capability, resilience or wellbeing must improve.
4. PRIDE != PROPAGANDA. National pride should be grounded in real qualities, histories and contributions, not inflated claims or comparative superiority.
5. CULTURE != COMMODITY. Visibility and tourism can damage cultural practices or places if volume becomes the objective.
6. WELLBEING != PRODUCTIVITY INSTRUMENT. Wellbeing matters intrinsically and cannot be justified only because it yields more labour/productivity.
7. PUBLIC PROCUREMENT != INDUSTRIAL POLICY BY DEFAULT. Procurement must remain lawful, quality-sensitive, transparent and proportionate.
8. HISTORICAL CONTEXT MATTERS. Present capacities and sensitivities are shaped by political, economic, cultural, colonial/postcolonial, migration, labour, institutional and regional histories. A policy may fail if this inherited context is ignored.

## Historian function — added to fixed competence core

The fixed team candidate requires historical competence, not occasional background fact-checking.

Recommended historical roles:

- State/institutional historian — development of state capacity, administrative traditions, constitutional/political settlements, public institutions and prior reform cycles.
- National/social historian — formation of national identity, migration, class, labour, regional relationships, crises and collective memory.
- Cultural historian — living traditions, symbols, food, dress, craft, celebration, music, media, education and changing meanings across generations.

For country work, these can be one person/agent if genuinely competent across all three; otherwise split adaptively.

### Historical intake questions

- What past institutions and reforms shaped present capacity?
- Which crises or successes remain active in public memory?
- What do current generations inherit materially, institutionally and symbolically?
- Which national/regional identities are complementary, contested or fragile?
- Which industries, crafts, foods, celebrations and institutions carry long historical continuity?
- Where did prior localisation, industrialisation, privatisation, austerity, protection or opening succeed/fail, and why?
- Which terms or symbols carry historical meanings that a foreign adviser may miss?
- What is remembered as dignity, betrayal, abundance, scarcity, progress or loss?

## Recommendation

Use `nasjonale og lokale trivselskretsløp` as an internal investigation field, not a proclaimed economic doctrine. Add the historian function permanently to the fixed competence-core candidate. Let Argentina be the first real case, but do not materially rewrite the Argentina booklet until this review is digested into a small number of evidence-backed changes.
