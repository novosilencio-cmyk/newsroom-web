# Argentina editorial team candidate 001

Status: INTERNAL CANDIDATE / NO PERMANENT STAFF EFFECT / NOT PUBLISHED

Purpose: provide culturally grounded, psychologically realistic and rhetorically strong review of Argentina public-guide and journalism work.

## Core adaptive team

- Argentina language & culture editor — project-specific specialist in Argentine Spanish, regional variation, cultural history, everyday language, social customs and federal diversity. First task is to review tone, voseo, idiom, cultural references and what feels authentically Argentine rather than generic Spanish-language copy.
- Psychologist — reviews motivation, belonging, curiosity, social enjoyment, age/life-stage realism, autonomy, shame/guilt risks and whether invitations genuinely support wellbeing rather than manipulate behaviour.
- Philosopher — reviews the underlying conception of good life, dignity, freedom, common good, intergenerational responsibility, beauty and the boundary between invitation and paternalism.
- Diplomat / Nansen — turns analysis into warm, respectful, non-preachy language suitable for public institutions and broad civic audiences; protects face, reciprocity and local ownership.
- Ariadne / language editor — clarity, rhythm, semantic precision and multi-register communication; works with the Argentina specialist rather than overriding local language.
- Societal-capacity analyst — checks that wellbeing, culture, production and participation are linked to real capacities and not only promotional language.
- Wildcard — searches for overlooked Argentine practices, regions, subcultures, minority/Indigenous perspectives, age groups and forms of enjoyment.

## Governing principles

1. Argentina expert leads cultural fit for Argentina-specific wording.
2. Spanish-language generalists may assist, but do not substitute for Argentina-specific review.
3. Do not stereotype life stages: young people, middle-aged and older people may all value celebration, friendship, nightlife, learning, dance, sport, food, nature and adventure.
4. Alcohol may be represented as part of adult cultural and gastronomic life where locally relevant, alongside non-alcoholic drinks, food, music and social settings; never frame alcohol as necessary for belonging or celebration.
5. Pride should arise from discovery of real cultural, social, scientific, natural and productive value — not superiority claims.
6. 'Mi Argentina' is a candidate civic-affective motif, not yet final copy.
7. The aim is invitation, discovery, health, joy, fascination, inspiration, belonging and participation without coercive behavioural manipulation.

## Evidence anchors already found

Argentina's own public sources describe festivals and events as expressions of cultural heritage with social and economic effects and contributions to federal identity and regional economies. The national cultural survey exists specifically to understand cultural habits and preferences across regions and age groups. Public heritage records treat celebration, food, dance, music, craft, learning and shared practices as living cultural identity. Tourism guidance already spans cultural, wine, nature, rural-productive, gastronomic, astro-, birding and hiking experiences.

These sources should be used as local starting material, not as proof that the proposed guide architecture is necessary or effective.

## Maturation rule

Use this team on Argentina work first. Do not create a generic Latin-America expert until repeated work shows that a cross-country layer is useful. Argentina-specific expertise should remain distinct from later country specialists.
