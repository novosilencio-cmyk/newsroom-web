Love me to lunch

Drawn by hand, transformed on screen, returned to paper in 2000.

Bjørn Moe Aldema

Hand drawing digitally processed in Photoshop.
Print from 2000.

A journey from paper to screen and back.

Bjørn Moe Aldema began with a drawing on paper. In Photoshop he mirrored and doubled its forms, changed their scale and added colour. The repeated lines brought the drawing into new company with itself.

The figures approach from several directions. They might be dancing, or deciding together where lunch should be.

The words are there in the picture: “Love me to lunch.”

There is room to linger over the invitation.

This photograph shows the print made in 2000. Creases and marks record some of the paper's life since then, joining the forms already on it.
