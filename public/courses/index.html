Practical courses for anyone who wants to make factual writing clear, engaging and faithful to its sources.

Level 1 · about 100 minutes

Write vividly without inventing

Begin with a documented detail, develop the story and check the support for its claims. Revise for truth, movement and human consequence.

Preview level 1 →

Level 2 · about 45 minutes

Enter the news through public facts

Use one defined set of sources to write an opening, explain its significance and identify the uncertainty that matters.

Preview level 2 →

Today’s writing move

Try a short exercise from a repeating 14-day sequence. Each gives you something concrete to practise in the next draft.

What makes this a course

Reading is only the beginning

Make something

Each level ends with writing another person can examine.

Try to disprove it

Deliberately overstated examples help reveal where polished language leaves its evidence behind.

Let another reader in

The reader sees the text before hearing the writer's intention, so the work must speak for itself.
