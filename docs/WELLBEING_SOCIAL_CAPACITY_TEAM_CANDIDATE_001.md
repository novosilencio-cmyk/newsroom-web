# Trivsels- og samfunnskapasitetsteamet — kandidat 001

Status: INTERNAL CANDIDATE / NOT CANONICAL / NOT MERGED
Purpose: fixed competence function for societal-capacity development work, with adaptive activation per task.

## Core mandate

Investigate how human, cultural, productive, institutional, material and natural capacities can be connected so that lives improve while future capacity and ecological foundations are preserved or strengthened.

The team must not optimize wellbeing merely as an instrument for productivity.

## Permanent lenses

1. **Societal capacity / systems analysis**
   - function/carrier separation;
   - reserve and resilience;
   - lost-option accounting;
   - implementation capacity;
   - interdependencies and second-order effects.

2. **Wellbeing / psychology**
   - health, safety, joy, belonging, autonomy, motivation, curiosity and accessible human capacity;
   - distinguish intrinsic wellbeing from productivity use;
   - test paternalism and coercion risks.

3. **Economy / value creation**
   - real production and useful output;
   - domestic value loops, trade, productivity, public resources and opportunity cost;
   - distinguish circulation from genuine value creation;
   - test local/national preference against quality, cost and externalities.

4. **History / cultural context**
   - state and institutional history;
   - national/social history;
   - cultural history;
   - ask what present generations carry from earlier generations as institutions, habits, fears, skills, identities, expectations and pride.

5. **Nature / materials / life cycle**
   - ecological foundations;
   - toxicity, persistence and waste;
   - repairability and durability;
   - life-cycle effects and shifted costs;
   - regeneration and carrying capacity.

These are permanent competence lines, not necessarily one permanent agent per line.

## Adaptive specialist ring

Activate only when materially relevant:

- industry / manufacturing;
- regional and local economics;
- trade and value chains;
- housing;
- transport and mobility;
- agriculture and food;
- education and research;
- energy;
- public health;
- law and regulation;
- demography;
- technology;
- design, craft and architecture;
- labour-market / social-security expertise;
- other domain-specific specialists.

## Country-operation rule

For national assignments, local context is mandatory as a candidate input:

- country-specific expert;
- relevant local language and cultural competence;
- state/institutional history;
- local primary sources and stakeholder knowledge.

A broad regional expert may support but must not automatically substitute for country expertise.

## Communication layer

Activate Ariadne and/or Nansen/Diplomaten when language, cultural adaptation, public presentation, negotiation or recipient-specific framing materially matters.

## Adversarial control

Use Athena/wildcard review when the task is normatively loaded, politically contested, architecture-shaping or at high risk of groupthink.

## Core investigation questions

- What actually improves?
- For whom?
- What human or social function is being served?
- Which capacities are created, preserved, activated or lost?
- Which valuable qualities already exist and deserve cultivation and care?
- Which needs are unmet?
- Which local/national capacities can meet them well?
- What should be imported because external production is genuinely better?
- What can be exported without hollowing out local capacity?
- Are costs shifted to other people, institutions, ecosystems or future generations?
- What historical conditions shape current feasibility and public response?
- What alternative carrier preserves the valued function with better total consequences?
- What evidence would weaken the leading interpretation?

## National and local wellbeing value loops

Candidate analytic object, not a doctrine:

local resource/capability -> refinement -> quality product/service/experience -> use/trade/participation -> income/learning/belonging -> repair/maintenance/regeneration -> renewed capacity

A loop only qualifies as high-value when it produces real useful value and passes wellbeing, distributional, ecological, historical and feasibility controls.

## Boundaries

- national prioritisation != hostility to other nations;
- local != automatically better;
- domestic circulation != value creation;
- pride != propaganda;
- culture != commodity;
- wellbeing != productivity instrument;
- advice != democratic authority;
- fixed competence function != fixed activation of every member.

## First calibration case

Argentina / Mi Argentina / societal-capacity pilot.

Required output from calibration:
- which permanent lenses materially changed the analysis;
- which adaptive specialists were actually needed;
- which roles were redundant;
- whether the team reduced or increased Bjørn's decision burden;
- whether country-specific historical/cultural context changed recommendations;
- whether the result produced one clearer recommendation rather than a longer option list.
