Status: INTERNAL TEST / NOT PUBLISHED / NOT SENT
Purpose: first real use of the fixed-competence/adaptive-team candidate before any permanent OOS anchoring.

## Work object

Improve the Argentina / Mi Argentina concept without allowing the new team architecture to inflate the booklet or displace the main journalistic and public-purpose goals.

## Permanent-lens contributions

### Societal capacity / systems
Adds explicit control for whether a proposal activates real capability, preserves future options, strengthens connections and remains implementable.

### Wellbeing / psychology
Keeps joy, social life, fascination, inspiration, belonging, autonomy and safety as intrinsic values. Prevents turning people into productivity units. Broadens discovery beyond tourism consumption into learning, participation, celebration, rest, making and social connection.

### Economy / value creation
Distinguishes local spending from genuine value creation. Adds quality, durability, repair, useful production, local refinement and domestic demand without assuming national production always wins.

### History / cultural context
Requires Argentina-specific state, national/social and cultural history before strong national-policy recommendations. Treats present attitudes to state, currency, public provision, work, migration, identity, regionality, celebration and national pride as historically carried context rather than free-floating preferences.

### Nature / materials / life cycle
Adds protection against local-production romanticism. Requires lifecycle, toxicity, durability, ecological carrying capacity and shifted-cost checks.

## Three high-value changes to the booklet

### 1. Move from category catalogue to human discovery modes
Primary interface candidates:

- I want to celebrate
- I want to dance
- I want to taste
- I want to learn
- I want to make something
- I want to meet people
- I want to be outside
- I want to rest
- I want to delight children
- I want to discover something unexpected nearby
- I want to find something well made in Argentina

Age remains a secondary practical filter, not the organising worldview.

### 2. Add 'Made here / made well' as a quality-discovery layer
Possible categories:

- clothing and textiles;
- bags and leather goods;
- jewellery;
- footwear;
- furniture;
- ceramics, glass, wood and metal;
- instruments;
- books and art;
- food and non-alcoholic beverages;
- wine and other alcoholic regional products for adults where culturally relevant;
- household products and repairable goods.

Selection must be based on evidence of quality, usefulness, provenance and appropriate material/lifecycle performance — not nationality alone.

### 3. Treat distribution as attention redirection
Do not limit distribution to public institutions. Evaluate high-footfall places where time and spending choices already occur:

- shopping centres;
- commercial streets;
- markets;
- transport hubs;
- festivals;
- sports and cultural venues;
- large workplaces/campuses;
- bookstores/cafes and other appropriate gathering places.

Candidate messages should redirect attention positively toward worthwhile Argentine alternatives, not shame imported consumption.

## Mi Argentina — emotional architecture

Candidate movement:

DISCOVER -> VALUE -> CARE FOR -> CULTIVATE / NURTURE

National prioritisation is legitimate and does not imply hostility to others. The safeguard is respect, rights, truthfulness and avoidance of needless harm — not a presumption that national pride is suspicious.

## Historical-context requirement before strong policy claims

For each major proposal ask:

- What similar policy or institution has Argentina tried before?
- Which crises, successes or betrayals shape public expectations now?
- What do current generations carry from previous inflationary, political, migration, labour, welfare and educational experiences?
- Which regional histories make one national policy land differently in different provinces?
- Which cultural practices are living rather than merely heritage-labelled?
- Which symbols of national pride are broadly shared, contested or region-specific?

## Negative controls

Reject or revise a proposal if it:

- creates spending without useful value;
- protects low quality only because it is domestic;
- commercialises culture in ways local communities reject;
- creates ecological pressure greater than the local benefit;
- treats wellbeing as a productivity lever;
- stereotypes life stages;
- uses national pride to erase internal diversity;
- ignores historical reasons for distrust or resistance;
- increases Bjørn's decision burden instead of reducing it.
