What your contribution makes possible

Support funds Experimental Newsroom’s research, documentation, source work, publishing and necessary operating costs. More stable support can make room for a workspace, paid contributors and, when finances allow, employed journalists.

Editorial independence

Your contribution is voluntary and does not buy goods, special access or editorial influence. The newsroom chooses its own stories, sources and conclusions.

Refunds and payment errors

You can request a full refund within 14 days of a one-time payment. Provide the date, amount and enough information to identify the payment. Refunds go to the original payment method, normally within 14 days of receiving your request. Contact us about duplicate payments or incorrect amounts too.
