For human writers

Write with life.
Keep your footing.

A small course sequence for journalists, editors, researchers, public communicators and anyone who wants factual writing to move without making more of the evidence than it can bear.

Level 1 · about 100 minutes

Write vividly without inventing

Find a documented anchor, build a truthful movement, map the status of every important claim and revise in three distinct passes.

Preview level 1 →

Level 2 · about 45 minutes

Enter the news through public facts

Write a lead, a nut paragraph and an uncertainty line from one bounded source set—without letting urgency outrun verification.

Daily practice · 5–15 minutes

Today’s writing move

One compact exercise each day from a transparent 14-day rotation. Small enough to do; precise enough to change the next draft.

What makes this a course

Reading is only the beginning

Make something

Each level ends in an observable piece of work, not a feeling that the material was understood.

Try to disprove it

Negative controls expose polished writing that has lost contact with its sources.

Let another reader in

Feedback is separated from the writer’s intention so the text has to work on its own.
