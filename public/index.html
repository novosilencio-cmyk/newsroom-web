Analysis

A document is where the trail starts

A budget can show that money was set aside. A law can show that a rule changed. We follow what happens next: who has to act, what reaches people or places, what changes, and which claims still need proof.

Constructive criticism

A promising idea gets harder questions

If something looks useful, we want to know how it works in practice, who benefits, what it costs, what becomes harder or remains unresolved, and what evidence would make us change our mind. Constructive does not mean uncritical.

Art

Human–AI collaboration

Art can open a way into a story that explanation alone cannot. We use original work, photography, human–AI collaboration and visual experiments when form helps the reader see the material more clearly. Imagination may shape the form; it does not turn an invented detail into a documented fact.

Atriet

A room that begins before the building

Atriet can begin on a path between the trees, beside the warmth of a fire, or at the edge of a screen where nothing immediately asks anything of the person who arrives.

We are developing Atriet as a physical and relational place for art, learning, work and encounters allowed to take the time they need.

This first visual study is a trace of that work — not a claim that the room is finished.

A place already alive, with room still left for whoever arrives.

About us

The newsroom, its people and how it works

Experimental Newsroom is an evolving journalistic and artistic collaboration between humans and AI. We work from real sources, keep uncertainty visible, and try to make responsibility traceable instead of hiding it behind the tools.

People

Editors, writers, researchers, technical contributors and specialist roles bring different kinds of judgment to the same work.

How we work

We distinguish what was documented, what a source claims, what we infer and what we still do not know — then follow what people and institutions actually do.

The experiment

We test whether serious reporting can be more vivid, curious and inviting without becoming less exact.

Reader support

Help sustain what you want to see realised

Visitors will be able to support Atriet, independent journalism, or the work as a whole. Contributions will be tracked by purpose and the public explanation will stay simple.
