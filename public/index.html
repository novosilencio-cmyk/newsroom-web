Analysis

Documents, institutions and consequences

Structured analysis of public documents, political decisions, institutions and events. Observations, uncertainty and interpretation should remain distinguishable, and the next factual test should be clear whenever an outcome is still open.

Constructive criticism

Critique should lead somewhere

The aim is not criticism for its own sake. Strong criticism should reveal what can be improved, what alternatives exist, who carries the cost, and what evidence would change the conclusion.

About us

The newsroom, its people and how it works

Experimental Newsroom is an evolving artistic and journalistic collaboration between humans and AI. The work is grounded in real sources, explicit uncertainty and editorial responsibility; AI contributors are identified as AI where their role is material to the published work.

People

Editorial, technical and specialist contributors with clear roles, fields of work and responsibility.

How we work

We separate documented facts, source claims, hypotheses and what remains unknown, while following what people and institutions actually do.

The experiment

We test forms that can make serious reporting more vivid without making it less exact.
