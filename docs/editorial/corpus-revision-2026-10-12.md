## Scope and authority

The author requested a Sjeherasad reading of all published texts, followed by sequential work with the editorial functions and an update of tags and search. The existing instruction authorizes publication after editorial completion. Sjeherasad is a bounded storytelling function. Four separate AI readers evaluated the corpus; the executing assistant selected and integrated revisions using Ariadne, followed by a separate AI integration review. This is not human specialist or native-speaker certification.

The frozen baseline is main `0554556fe94ebfa18a3ccf319b7ae83c68aa8cdd`. The corpus contains 25 published stories in 41 article HTML files and 30 other HTML pages. All were read, including embedded language panels, all 14 daily exercises and the embedded artwork controls. Unpublished work is outside this release.

## Six stages

1. **Helhetsinntrykk:** assess each complete text and its reader journey before selecting changes. The corpus combines news, analysis, essay, project notes, art, instruction and functional pages.
2. **Styrker:** preserve sustained reasoning, concrete openings, source distinctions, authorial voice and genre differences. Revision does not require rewriting a sound text.
3. **Stilprofil:** retain long-form depth where it earns its place; neither fragment every article nor add invented scenes. Preserve the author's origin and hypothetical framing.
4. **Konkrete svakheter:** identify the individual issues in the table below. Two published Virkemidlene stories were absent from the main discovery register.
5. **Forbedringsgrep:** apply the selected exact replacements one story at a time; add appropriately scoped revision/correction notes and grounded Norwegian/English topics. Preserve original publication and source-check dates.
6. **Revidert eksempelversjon:** the changed public HTML files are the reviewed versions. Metadata-only changes retain the existing body. Final receipts bind each changed page to its exact SHA-256.

## Semantic control

The material corrections distinguish percentage points from relative percentages and remove the reversed meaning of the memory sentence; each has a visible correction note. Participation retains democratic value independently of practical benefit. Group patterns remain uncertain descriptions of individuals. FinCEN investigation remains evidence-dependent. French participation wording follows the existing English suitability distinction. English Økotun wording distinguishes co-design from operational responsibility. German essay meaning and all authorial afterword material are preserved, including the Arabic language choice.

This is an editorial and discovery revision, not a fresh factual investigation of every article. The sources retain their original dates and limitations. No claim is made that old calls remain open, forecasts occurred, merger status is unchanged, or legal and financial findings have been freshly verified. Source-link recovery for the procurement article and fresh regulatory checks remain separate reporting follow-ups. An attempted EUR-Lex reread of the existing AI Act amendment citation returned a robot-verification page; the amendment/date claim was not independently revalidated in this revision. No factual update is claimed for it.
