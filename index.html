Analysis

Documents, institutions and consequences

Structured analysis of public documents, political decisions, institutions and events. Observations, uncertainty, interpretation and recommendations should remain distinguishable.

Constructive criticism

Critique should lead somewhere

The aim is not criticism for its own sake. Strong criticism should reveal what can be improved, what alternatives exist, and what evidence would change the conclusion.

Services

Analysis, agent training and advisory work

Potential services include research, agent evaluation and training, selected courses, document analysis and carefully bounded personal advisory services.
