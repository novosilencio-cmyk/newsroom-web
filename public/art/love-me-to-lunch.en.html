Love me to lunch

A drawing took a trip through Photoshop.
In 2000, it was back on paper.

Bjørn Moe Aldema

Hand drawing digitally processed in Photoshop.
Print from 2000.

From paper to screen.
From screen to paper.

It began with a drawing on paper. Bjørn Moe Aldema took it into Photoshop, where the forms were mirrored, duplicated, resized and given colour. A line found company in itself.

In the image, the curious figures meet from several directions. Perhaps they are dancing. Perhaps they are trying to agree on where to eat.

“Love me to lunch,” it says.

An invitation you can take a little time with.

Shown here is a photograph of the print from 2000. The paper has gathered creases and marks along the way. Now they, too, are part of the picture.
