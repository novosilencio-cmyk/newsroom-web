Open call · Norwegian-language pilot

Help us test a writing course that refuses to invent

Help test a course in clear, vivid factual writing. We are seeking one participant and one separate reader to evaluate the work.

Course concept and original method: Bjørn Moe Aldema · Pilot preparation: Experimental Newsroom human–AI editorial collaboration · Published by Experimental Newsroom on 2 September 2026

The course explores how a text can feel alive while remaining faithful to its sources. You will work with concrete detail, a developing explanation and clear limits on what the material can support.

This first pilot takes place in Norwegian, although the invitation is in English. It uses a constructed, fictional source pack. Publication of a participant's writing would require a separate request and explicit consent.

Role one

Participant

The participant must be at least 18, able to read and write Norwegian and independent of the course's development. You receive the course and a defined source pack, then write a short factual text with supporting notes.

Time: about 100 minutes of individual work, followed by about 10 minutes of feedback.

Role two

Evaluator

The evaluator must be at least 18, able to work in Norwegian and independent of both course development and the participant's writing. Editing or source-assessment experience is useful.

Time: about 20 minutes for the first independent assessment.

What the pilot tests

We want to see whether the course helps a new reader build a text with a documented opening, a developing question, a turn and a meaningful return. Facts, source claims, interpretation and unknowns must remain distinguishable.

The evaluator assesses six dimensions and also reads an intentionally overstated calibration text. Accepting that text as accurate would expose a weakness in the evaluation, even if the main submission reads well.

Voluntary, unpaid and private by default

This first pilot is voluntary and unpaid. Expressing interest is not enrolment. Selected volunteers receive the full information and a separate consent step before anything begins. You may withdraw before or during the pilot.

Participation does not grant permission to publish your text, feedback, identity or contact details. Internal preservation of anonymised material is a separate choice. Any later public use requires a new request.
