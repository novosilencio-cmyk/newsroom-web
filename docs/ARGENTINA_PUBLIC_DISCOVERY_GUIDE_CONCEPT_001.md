# Argentina — public discovery guide concept 001

Status: INTERNAL PRODUCT CONCEPT / NOT PUBLISHED / NOT SENT
Intended owner if adopted: Argentine public authorities or a designated public-purpose partner.

## Purpose

Create a national guide that helps people discover what already exists across Argentina: culture, learning, nature, food, craft, local production, places, events and people worth seeking out.

The guide should support pride without propaganda, curiosity without consumerism for its own sake, and local economic activity without turning every place into a tourism commodity.

## Core invitation

See what we have.
Discover where it is.
Meet the people who know and make things.
Learn something.
Travel further.
Buy and use local when it is genuinely worthwhile.
Create something yourself.
Help build the country forward.

## Two-layer product

### 1. Physical public booklet

A well-designed printed guide that people can actually hold, keep, lend and use.

### 2. Living digital atlas

A continuously updated digital layer connected to the booklet by QR codes, short links and place identifiers.

The printed object provides discovery, orientation and cultural identity. The digital layer provides freshness, maps, schedules, transport, accessibility and deeper information.

## Distribution is part of the product

A guide that only exists as a PDF has failed this objective.

The physical booklet should be designed to reach people through places they already use:

- schools and universities;
- libraries;
- municipal and provincial offices;
- railway and bus stations;
- cultural centres;
- tourism offices;
- museums;
- public service locations where appropriate;
- community organisations;
- selected cafés, bookstores and locally rooted businesses;
- major national and regional events.

Distribution should include provinces and smaller communities, not only Buenos Aires.

Possible forms:
- free national edition;
- low-cost durable edition;
- regional inserts;
- school/youth edition;
- annual or seasonal editions;
- rotating thematic editions: food, craft, science, music, nature, rail journeys, thermal landscapes, etc.

## Feedback loop

People and communities should be able to propose additions and corrections.

Suggested flow:
local proposal -> provenance check -> editorial verification -> local/context review -> inclusion candidate -> publication/update.

This avoids a purely top-down national catalogue and lets the guide become a living map of Argentina's own capacities.

## Economic mechanism to test

visibility -> curiosity -> domestic travel -> local spending -> stronger local producers and culture -> more reasons to travel -> stronger regional identity and capacity.

This is a hypothesis to measure, not an assumed outcome.

Candidate measurements:
- visits to included places;
- spending retained locally;
- discovery of small producers;
- repeat domestic travel;
- public-transport use;
- participation in cultural/learning activities;
- seasonal distribution of visitors;
- local satisfaction;
- ecological pressure indicators.

## Editorial principles

- invitation, not propaganda;
- culture is not decoration;
- communities are not tourism products;
- local knowledge should be represented with provenance and respect;
- lesser-known places should have a path to visibility;
- commercial payment should not determine editorial inclusion;
- beauty, usefulness, learning and human experience matter alongside popularity;
- preserve what makes a place worth visiting rather than optimizing it for maximum visitor volume.

## Recommended pilot

Select 5–10 culturally and geographically different places. Build full living cards for each and compare the result against existing Argentine national and provincial tourism/culture platforms.

The pilot passes only if it demonstrates at least one of the following:
- materially better discovery of local cultural/productive capacity;
- stronger connections between places and domestic travel routes;
- useful learning/cultural content missing from existing guides;
- realistic benefit for local producers or institutions;
- a scalable data/editorial architecture without excessive local burden.

If the pilot adds little beyond existing services, do not scale it.
